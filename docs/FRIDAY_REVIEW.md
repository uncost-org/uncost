# Uncost Friday review

Approved design; staged rollout, not an active publication service.

- **v1:** Register review cards and a Saturday Health summary.
- **v2:** Cost Watch, only after two clean live Saturdays of v1.
- **v3:** Uncost News.

Each stage requires its own dry run and explicit founder go-live. Friday preparation is silent. Saturday review is delivered even when nothing needs a decision; source failures are reported honestly rather than presented as an empty successful review.

## Register review

Figures are **agent-checked, human-approved**. Each card presents the old and proposed value, period, units, region, exact public source, verification evidence and proposed change. Original publisher evidence must be checked; an unavailable source is not a verified number.

At most ten Register cards are considered per week, including pending cards. Normally the oldest checks come first. The first live batch prioritizes SRC-017, SRC-025, SRC-026, SRC-027, then SRC-024 within that cap. SRC-017 remains a weekly Register source with news-feed placement and is displayed only in Cost Watch, not durable sector headlines.

Approval binds exact content and evidence. The first valid action wins; repeated actions are already recorded. Silence never approves. Pending cards carry over, then expire after fourteen days with a Health notice. Changed figures need new evidence and approval. All four required Cost Watch prints are checked again at the publication gate.

Register changes remain blocked until the pending content integration and the separately reviewed attribution schema are merged. An approval is not permission to bypass source, schema, review or required-check safeguards.

## Later stages

[Cost Watch criteria](COST_WATCH_CRITERIA.md) govern eligibility and ranking. AP stays disabled; official releases are expected to provide most candidates. Cost Watch has a cap of five cards. **REPORTED** belongs only under `/news/`, never in the Register. Uncost News has a cap of one card, based on merged public changes rather than plans. A later rule-review feature has a cap of three proposed rules on the first Saturday of a month and needs separate authorization.

Rejection feedback can inform ranking, but cannot change criteria automatically. Edited text needs a new explicit approval. Approved changes use reviewed pull requests and verifiable receipts; no automatic approval is inferred from delivery or scoring.

The criteria file is preserved byte-for-byte from the approved design. Its original draft banner and references to the former detailed specification are historical text, not current rollout authority. This page describes public behavior; operational implementation and setup are not published here.
