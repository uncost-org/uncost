# POL-011 — Corrections & Source Integrity Policy

**Status:** reference reserved; drafting not started.

This file exists to reserve a permanent, citable reference number. It contains no
policy content, and none may be added while its status is `reserved`: drafting it
means writing it against the controlling source in `docs/CONTROL.md`, stamping its
derivation, adding it to the policy pack, and moving its status to `draft` — the
same route every other policy took.

**Why the number is reserved now.** The Receipts already commits publicly to a
corrections log, so the obligation is referenced on the site before the policy
governing it has been written. Reserving `POL-011` makes that gap citable instead
of silent.

**Scope, rules, and process are undrafted.** Nothing about how corrections will be
recorded, who approves them, or what integrity guarantees apply has been decided
here. Do not cite this file as authority for any of it.

**Contact:** questions about this reservation go to contact@uncost.org.
